ℹ️ About the Accessibility Data

AccessTour retrieves place information from online mapping and place-data services rather than requiring a separate HTML page for every destination.

Google Places currently provides several wheelchair-related accessibility fields for eligible places:

  • Wheelchair accessible entrance
  • Wheelchair accessible parking
  • Wheelchair accessible restroom
  • Wheelchair accessible seating

"Not reported" means the available place-data source did not provide a verified value. It does not necessarily mean that the facility is inaccessible.
